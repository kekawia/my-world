Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

My World is a personal app used only by its owner, on his own PC. This page says what it does with Google data.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads his Gmail accounts with read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission). It never sends, changes, moves, labels or deletes mail, and never marks mail as read.

What it keeps

On the owner's PC only: for each new mail, the sender, subject, date and Gmail's labels, and a short summary of the few mails worth his attention. The text of a mail is not stored. The sign-in pass for each account is kept on the same PC, locked with the owner's Windows sign-in.

Where it goes

To write the summaries, the text of a few mails is sent to the owner's own AI tool, through his own subscription. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

My World's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google Account's linked apps page. Removing an account in the app deletes what it kept for that account.
